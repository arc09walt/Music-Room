THE ROOM AND ITS SOUNDS

Backstage, the Music Room's kit, its toy shelf and
every struck voice in it are synthesized by the app
itself, from recipes original to this app's family
(Endless Beats / Beats, by Songwright), and no
third-party pattern library is included: the rhythm
you write is yours, written on a blank sixteen
squares.

On the stage (1.2; the full band seated in 1.3), the seated instruments
play from real recordings - the same single-note
anchors the family's performer rooms ship, credited
in the next section. The stage's lines are written
by you, or dealt by the podium's dealer under the
house's own arrangement rules; no performed lines
ship in this app.

THE STAGE'S RECORDED BANKS (1.2)

The anchors are single recorded notes and hits, one
file per pitch, played back note for note - the
nearest anchor to each written pitch, shifted by at
most a few semitones inside fences carried from the
rooms the banks came from. A note is not a
performance.

THE FULL BAND'S RECORDED BANKS (new in 1.3 - 299 anchors)

In 1.3 the docket's five families took their seats:
the whole stage now plays from real recordings, every
bank license-verified before it was staged. New banks
convert to the house format (mono 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis;
sustains trimmed to 2.2 seconds with a faded tail, the
house's brass convention; decays capped at 5 seconds).
Pitch truth in these banks was MEASURED on the staged
files by FFT pitch check, not assumed from filenames:
the VSCO-2 CE filenames carry Yamaha octave numbers
(their "C3" is middle C), and the anchors below stand
at the sounding pitches.

Woodwinds (47 anchors) - VSCO-2 CE, Versilian
Studios, CC0 1.0:
  Flute (10) - sustain, non-vibrato first.
  Oboe (9) - the vibrato set (judged true oboe).
  Clarinet (11) - long sustain; the soprano clarinet
  is a transposing instrument and these anchors stand
  at SOUNDING pitch, as written music expects them
  played.
  Bassoon (17) - the vibrato set first.

Bowed strings (123 anchors):
  Solo Violin (15) - VSCO-2 CE, CC0 1.0, arco
  vibrato, the forte layer.
  Viola (46) and Cello (46) - University of Iowa
  Musical Instrument Samples (MIS), created by
  Lawrence Fritts; the library's own terms are
  quoted above (they "may be downloaded and used
  for any projects, without restrictions"). These
  recordings are not CC0 and this notice does not
  call them that.
  Double Bass arco (16) and pizzicato (11) -
  D. Smolken Double Bass, Karoryfer Samples, CC0
  1.0. (The pizzicato set's staged name said
  "SF Salamander" while its maker manifest says
  Smolken; the SFZ played in the stage is
  Smolken's, and this credit follows the manifest.)

Harp (23 anchors) - VSCO-2 CE Concert Harp (the
KSHarp set), CC0 1.0.

Timpani (6 anchors - three drums, hits and rolls) -
Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL) Timpani,
CC0 1.0; drum pitches from the library's own SFZ
mapping (D2 / A2 / F3).

Harpsichord (28 anchors) - VCSL Harpsichord,
English, Normal stop, CC0 1.0.

THE FAMILIES IN THE WORKS - FULFILLED IN 1.3

Every family that stood on the 1.2 stage plan
tagged IN THE WORKS has now earned its seat under
the rule that governed them: recordings passed the
license gate first (above). The docket stands empty.
The standing ruling that rode with the keyboard's
tag rides on, fulfilled as written: the piano remains
ruled out of this house's library as a flowing
instrument, and the keyboard chairs seated in 1.3
are struck-and-plucked voices, never a piano chair
under another name.

THE UNLOCK KEYS

Unlock keys are Ed25519-signed tokens minted by the
developer's own watcher. Verification inside the app is
the app's own code (a compact pure-JavaScript Ed25519
check, written for this app). The public key embedded
in the app is published with the watcher; the private
key never leaves the developer's machine.
